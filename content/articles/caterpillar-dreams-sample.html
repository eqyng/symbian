031225

Step inside the mushroom ring And find yourself turned around Or fall asleep upon grassy knolls And you may never be found

Handfuls of moss and mint underfoot Small ghoulish glows in the mist Where doth the little forest fairies go With the children we've lost and miss?

100717

Chew on words like tacky taffy Make them into bubblegum Find them stuck behind your teeth Caught where you can't reach

Chomp into vowels, and Cough up consonants Stretch letters around inscisors Loop shapes around your tongue, or

Coat your gyms with sounds Feed on phrases in strips Snack on half-baked thoughts, Guide them past your lips
