Navigating the symbiotic
human-machine relationship.

How can we better anticipate consequences, gauge emergent risks and develop solutions while we still have the time?

The more we become entangled with technology, the more urgent this question becomes.

I'm a conversation designer with a background in psychology and an interest in topics like governance, cybersecurity and human-computer interactions. I have contributed to IEEE working groups in emulated empathy in AI companions, worked in industry and government.

I write about the gap between what innovation promises, and how it unintentionally impacts us. These problems don't have clean, reassuring, absolute answers. They need us to hold contradictions in tension and have the patience, resourcefulness and creativity to navigate this space.

Hope you see what I see.
