About

Navigating the symbiosis of human-machine relationships.

Welcome to a cultural biography of our evolving, symbiotic relationship with technology.

As a conversation designer with a background in psychology and interests spanning AI governance, adversarial dialogue systems and human-agent interaction, I'm passionate about emergent, structural issues stemming from emotional AI.

The questions I explore are related to the the promises of technology and the reality of impact on humans, the wonders of possibility and theunintentional longterm harm we might be causing. I ask: how can we better anticipate consequences, gauge risk and and develop solutions while we have the time?

In this future, I believe nuance, patience and resourcefulness are necessary tools to help us navigate an unpredictable, complex landscape. We must be able to hold contradictions in tension rather than rushing to fill that disquieting space with reassuring absolutes. We need to sit at the intersection to see the whole picture.

If you're also interested in the edges, you're in the right place.
